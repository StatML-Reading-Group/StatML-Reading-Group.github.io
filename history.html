History

The Statistics and Machine Learning Reading Group was founded in 2009–2010 by Larry Wasserman, John Lafferty and Aarti Singh, and has met most weeks of term ever since.

For its first years the group read a paper each week, with a designated presenter and often a commenter — the pre-2015 entries in the archive still record the paper rather than an abstract. Over time the format shifted toward members presenting their own work in progress.

Until July 2025 the site lived at statml.cs.cmu.edu, served from an AFS directory on CMU's Andrew servers and deployed by hand over SSH. When CMU SCS stopped serving Andrew-cell content that summer, the site went dark and stayed dark. It was rebuilt in 2026 from the group's own records; nothing was lost except one abstract from February 2022, whose page was never generated.

Earlier research themes

Between roughly 2012 and 2015 the group organised itself around a handful of recurring themes: active and compressive learning, adaptive nonparametric estimation, sparse PCA, statistical testing in high dimensions, and topological data analysis. Those pages are preserved in the repository's _archive/ directory along with the publication list from that era.
