About

We are a group of faculty, postdocs and students in Statistics and Machine Learning at Carnegie Mellon, broadly interested in research at the intersection of the two.

We meet weekly during term. One member presents a paper or their own work, and we discuss it. Talks range from theory to methods to applications; everyone is welcome, and you do not need to be from either department to come along.
