About this archive

Talks from Fall 2010 onward, reconstructed from the group's own records: the previous site's archive pages, per-semester schedule spreadsheets, and the original pre-2015 pages hosted by Aarti Singh. Pre-2015 entries record the paper the group read rather than an abstract — in that era this was a reading group in the literal sense.

The group's tenth-anniversary slides record its founding: 2009–2010, by Larry Wasserman, John Lafferty and Aarti Singh.
